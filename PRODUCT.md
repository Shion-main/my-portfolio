# Product

> Current scope (2026-10-13): a dark portfolio featuring a scroll-driven collage of Space Arabica and Korte, followed by focused project previews and case studies. It serves freelance clients and hiring teams equally. Joshua confirmed that he designed and built both himself. Public copy emphasizes design and development. See [the current design contract](docs/designs/scroll-collage-portfolio.md) and `src/content.ts`; older scope notes below are historical.

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring teams and freelance clients evaluating Joshua Sabuero's design and front-end work. They need to understand his strengths, see credible project evidence, and reach him quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Joshua's work as a UI/UX designer and front-end developer, grounded in the projects, experience, and credentials documented in his resume. Success means a visitor can understand his role, scan representative projects, and start a conversation without friction.

## Brand Personality

Confident, crafted, community-minded. The site should feel capable and direct, with the human context of each project visible.

## Anti-references

Avoid the previous neon glow, decorative glass panels, generic full-stack claims, moving carousels, and vague technology marketing copy.

## Design Principles

1. Show actual work and clearly identify Joshua's contribution.
2. Give hiring teams and clients a fast path from introduction to projects to contact.
3. Keep claims traceable to the resume or visible project artifacts.
4. Use strong visual hierarchy without sacrificing readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, meaningful image descriptions, and a reduced-motion alternative.
