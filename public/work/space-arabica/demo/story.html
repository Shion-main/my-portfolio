ONE FOUNDER.
EVERY DETAIL.

Space Arabica is owned and run by its founder and general manager, Gilda Elaiza D. Cinco.

She studied Mass Communication and earned her MBA at the University of Mindanao. Her 2019 graduate research looked at what customers value, and that thinking shaped the café: she compared locations and surveyed people on price, facilities, ambiance, and experience before a single table was built.

From question to counter

HOW IT
CAME TOGETHER.

  1. 2019

    A question about people

    Graduate research on what customers value — location, price, facilities, ambiance — later shapes the business plan.

  2. 2022

    Finding the place

    Locations are compared and surveys run on pricing, facilities, ambiance, and experience. The concept takes shape with architects and builders.

  3. Nov 2022

    Doors open in Ecoland

    Space Arabica opens on Ecowest Drive: good coffee, shared tables, fast Wi-Fi, and a mini conference room.

  4. Today

    Still evolving

    Nine signature episodes and counting, two bookable spaces, and a team trained in-house.
